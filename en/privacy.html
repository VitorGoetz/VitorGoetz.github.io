Privacy Policy

SortFilme is an app that randomly picks movies and TV shows. Using the app does not require sign-up or login. Signing in with your Google account is optional and is only used to save your lists to your account. This policy explains which data is processed when you use the app.

1. Data stored on your phone

Your “To watch” and “Watched” lists, the ratings and comments you write about titles (Premium), filters and preferences are saved on your device. Without signing in, the lists are not sent to any of our servers; they may be copied by your phone's Google automatic backup, according to your Android settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device.

1.1. Google account (optional)

If you tap “Sign in with Google”, we receive your e-mail address, the name and the profile photo link of your Google account and an account identifier from Google. The name and photo are shown only to you, in your Profile inside the app; the e-mail and identifier are used to keep your lists in your account. We use them to create your account on our server (Supabase) and store your lists in it (titles, whether you watched them, their order, your ratings and comments and the dates you added or changed them), so they come back when you change phones or reinstall the app. We do not receive your password or any other Google account data. This data is used only for this purpose: it is not sold, shared with third parties or used for ads. Communication is encrypted (HTTPS) and each account can only access its own lists.

Deleting your account: in the app, in the “Profile” tab, tap “Delete account”. The account and all lists saved in it are permanently deleted right away. You can also request deletion without the app: how to delete your account and data. Signing out removes the lists from this device but does not delete the account data: they come back when you sign in again.

1.2. Award Night (optional)

In Award Night (group movie voting), whoever creates a room gets a code and other people join with it, each on their own phone and signed in. For the room to work, we store on our server your first name (from your Google account) and your vote. Other people in the room see only your first name, whether you have voted and the titles in the draw: those in the “To watch” lists of the people in the room that nobody there has watched, with how many people want each one, without saying whose title it is (nobody sees your “Watched” list, your ratings, your e-mail or your photo). The room and this data are deleted after 45 minutes without activity (at most 3 hours after it is created), when its creator leaves or when the account is deleted. After that we keep only an anonymous record of the session, with no names (date, number of people, the drawn titles, the votes and the chosen title), linked to the accounts that took part, for app statistics; it is deleted after 12 months or when the account is deleted. To create rooms, our server checks your Premium purchase with Google Play (we keep only a scrambled code of the purchase, never payment data).

1.3. Reminders (optional)

If you turn on the “Draw reminder” (from the in-app invite or in Profile), your phone shows a heads-up on Wednesdays and Saturdays at 5:30 PM. The reminder is scheduled only on your device: no data is sent to our server or to third parties, and no external notification service is used. You can turn it off anytime in Profile or in Android's notification settings.

2. Movie and TV catalog

To pick a title, the app queries our catalog (hosted on Supabase). The request only contains the chosen filters (type, genre, year, rating and language). As with any internet request, the server technically receives your IP address, which we do not use to identify you.

If the app hits an error, it sends a technical report to the same server: app and Android version, device model, language and the error description. The report contains no name, e-mail, account, location or device identifier, and is used only to fix problems. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's SDK may collect the device advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device information and ad interactions to serve, measure and prevent fraud in ads. On first launch, where required by law (e.g. in Europe), the app shows Google's consent message, and you can change your choice under “Ad privacy”. You can also reset or disable the advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more: How Google uses data from partner apps.

4. Premium purchase (Google Play)

The Premium purchase is made and processed by Google Play. We do not receive payment data. The app only checks with Google Play whether your account has bought Premium. So that Premium works on your SortFilme account, the purchase carries a scrambled account code (no e-mail or name); that is why you need to sign in with Google to buy or use Premium.

5. Rewarded video

When you watch a video to unlock the lists for 1 hour, item 3 (Google AdMob ads) applies.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Third-party content

Synopses and images come from freely licensed sources (Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and TVmaze), credited in the app. “Read more” links open those websites, which have their own policies.

8. Your rights

Without signing in, we do not keep your personal data on our servers. If you sign in, you can see your lists in the app and delete the account and all its data at any time (item 1.1). Questions or requests about your data can be sent through the developer contact on the SortFilme page on Google Play.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
