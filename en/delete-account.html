Delete your account and data

A SortFilme account only exists if you signed in with Google to save your lists to the cloud. Without signing in, there is no account or personal data on our servers.

In the app (immediately)

  1. Open SortFilme and tap the Profile tab.
  2. In the Account section (where your e-mail is shown), tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Without the app

Send a request to the developer contact e-mail shown on the SortFilme page on Google Play (“App support” section), with the subject “Delete account”, from the same e-mail as the Google account used in the app. Deletion is completed within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • The account (e-mail, name, profile photo link and Google account identifier).
  • All lists saved in the account (“To watch” and “Watched”), including your ratings and comments.

Everything is permanently deleted; we keep no copy. When you delete it in the app, the lists are also erased from your phone. The Premium purchase stays recorded on Google Play, but it is linked to this SortFilme account: after deleting it, Premium no longer works in the app, even if you create a new account. Technical error reports contain no account data and are deleted after 90 days.
